=== Digha ===
images: digha-1.jpg, digha-2.jpg

Digha is one of Bengal's most-loved sea-side getaways, just a short drive from Kolkata. Soft sands, gentle surf and rows of casuarina trees make it a perfect weekend escape. Our package includes beachfront stays, sunrise walks at New Digha and a side trip to the quiet shores of Mandarmani.

=== Sundarban ===
images: sundarban-1.jpg, sundarban-2.jpg, sundarban-3.jpg

Cruise through the world's largest mangrove forest in search of the elusive Royal Bengal Tiger. Our Sundarban expedition includes licensed launches, watchtower visits at Sajnekhali and Dobanki, village folk performances and freshly cooked Bengali meals on board.

=== Kolkata ===
images: kolkata-1.jpg, kolkata-2.jpg

The City of Joy comes alive on our two-day heritage walk: Howrah Bridge at dawn, the colonial corridors of BBD Bagh, Kumartuli's clay artisans, College Street's book bazaar and an evening of Rabindra Sangeet on the Hooghly. Street food trails included.

=== Murshidabad ===
images: murshidabad-1.jpg

Step into the lost capital of Bengal's Nawabs. Wander Hazarduari Palace, the Katra Mosque and Nizamat Imambara, and sail down the Bhagirathi at sunset. A slow, quiet trip rich in history and silk-weaving traditions.

=== Darjeeling ===
images: darjeeling-1.jpg, darjeeling-2.jpg, darjeeling-3.jpg

Watch the first light hit Kanchenjunga from Tiger Hill, ride the heritage Toy Train to Ghoom, sip first-flush tea at a working estate and lose yourself in the prayer-flagged calm of Peace Pagoda. Our Darjeeling tour blends iconic sights with offbeat hamlets like Lamahatta and Tinchuley.
